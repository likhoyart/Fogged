# Visual direction — v0.6.2

The requested reference is **Silent Hill: Homecoming**, specifically its PS3-era presentation, not PS1 vertex jitter or a CRT treatment.

References consulted:

- [Homecoming PS3 screenshot gallery](https://www.mobygames.com/game/37545/silent-hill-homecoming/screenshots/ps3/), including the [foggy street frame](https://www.mobygames.com/game/37545/silent-hill-homecoming/screenshots/ps3/523836/).
- [Homecoming screenshots](https://steamcommunity.com/app/19000/screenshots/), including residential exteriors.
- [GameSpot's 2008 hands-on report](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/e3-2008-silent-hill-homecoming-updated-hands-on/1100-6193990/), describing the empty night streets, vehicles, lighting and fog.

Art-direction interpretation for this prototype: restrained gray-green shadows, warm localized light, low saturation, legible nearby facade textures and silhouettes that lose contrast into the fog. Use ordinary building forms and texture detail instead of large polygon counts. The default 720p render target, stronger vignette and subtle grain support the console-era direction without deliberate geometry wobble.

The other palettes are optional interpretations, not claims about the original game's exact color pipeline. Final grading and model silhouette fidelity still need a GPU-enabled visual comparison. Reference screenshots are linked, not copied into the game.

Handheld flashlight research: Flash-Lite by AndrewHunt95 was considered but its download could not be obtained through the available browser; the Dango Games page was rate-limited; Void Frame Studio's model had a no-derivatives license. The implemented replacement is the [free Flashlight by Glitch5970](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/flashlight-8c82abdd1f394d2f8ac3c1a843796b4a), CC BY 4.0, checked in its download dialog. No paid model is included.
